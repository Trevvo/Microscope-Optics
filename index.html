Warnings

Model & assumptions
  • Excitation: each LED switched on in an acquisition delivers equal integrated optical power; spectra are converted to photon flux (∝ P·λ). Paddle filter → cube exciter → cube dichroic reflection.
  • Fluorophores are all treated as equally bright: extinction coefficient and quantum yield are not used. Absorption ∝ ∫ (photon flux at sample × peak-normalised excitation spectrum); emission follows the area-normalised emission spectrum.
  • Dichroics are lossless: R = 1 − T. Gemini: transmitted light → Camera 1, reflected → Camera 2.
  • Detection = cube dichroic T × cube emitter × Gemini split × arm filter × camera QE. An empty slot passes everything (T = 1); a missing camera counts as QE = 1.
  • 1-photon excitation far from saturation; no bleaching, FRET, or environment effects; objective, tube lens and collection NA treated as flat across wavelength.
  • Exc. eff. = fraction of LED photons that excite the fluorophore as effectively as photons at its excitation peak. Collected = fraction of emitted photons that are detected.
  • Excitation leakage = fraction of excitation photons at the sample that would reach a camera if back-reflected (upper bound).
  • Spectra from an FPbase snapshot, resampled to 300–1000 nm at 1 nm; filters are T = 0 outside their measured range. SPECTRA X LEDs are the unfiltered sources (FPbase "Spectra X23", checked against Lumencor's published unfiltered output); FPbase's older "SpectraX" entries are filtered outputs and are flagged as such.
